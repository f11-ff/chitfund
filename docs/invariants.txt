
Invariants: Conditions for a valid system

Length of not winners must be equal to the difference between total months and the current month
Each month has exactly one winner
Member cannot win twice
Month increases only after valid winner assignment
Member Names must be unique
The payout for the Manager is constant (although not shown here)
The outstanding dues for member changes only after winning, and it increases only by 1 percent the chit amount
Each month maps exactly to only one winner
Current Month is always less than or equal to the total member count
